About Us

More than just a box. Marlowby is a connection between creators, communites, and curious consumers.

  • Our Mission - To celebrate local businesses,showcase the iniqueness of different communites, and make discovering new products an exciting experience.
  • Our Vision - To create a community where small businesses receive recongnition, local products reach new audiences, and customers can explore the country one box aat a time.

At Marlowby, we believe every community has something special to offer. From unique handmade products to local favorites, there are countless small busnisses and creators worth discovering. Each box introuces you to new places, local businesses, and products that tell a story about the communities they come from. Whether you're discovering a new favorites or learning about a city you've never visited, we want every box to be an experience worth looking foward to.

Local Businesses

Our local businesses come from all over the States! Here are four of our most popular partners.

Candle NYC

Handmade candles crafted to perfection

A family owned, hand poured, candle company born in New York. Every candle is made from a century-old secret family formula.

The Silver Room

Jewelery, Books, Art, and Incense

Their mission is to celebrate African American beauty, fashion, art, and culture while supporting local artists and Black-owned businesses on Chicago’s South Side.

Bloom

Handcrafted Beauty Products

Founded by founders Jamie and Joseph, this East End/Telephone Road-based business specializes in natural, handcrafted bath and body essentials, body scrubs, body oils, soy candles, and skincare.

The Cave of Wonders

Crystal boutique

A beloved metaphysical shop, gem store, and curiosity boutique located in San Diego's North Park neighborhood. Known for blending formal gemological expertise with a playful, welcoming atmosphere, it serves both serious crystal collectors and casual browsers looking for unique gifts.
